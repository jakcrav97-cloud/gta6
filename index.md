# GTA 6 guides

Last updated: 2026-11-18

Every published guide answers one question. Each answer below is the page's first line.

## Release
- [When is the GTA 6 release date?](guides/gta-6-release-date.md) — GTA 6 comes out on 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- [What time does GTA 6 come out in the UK?](guides/gta-6-release-time-uk.md) — GTA 6 comes out at midnight UK local time, as 18 November turns into 19 November 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- [What time does GTA 6 unlock?](guides/gta-6-unlock-time.md) — GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on 19 November 2026, in all regions, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- [Is GTA 6 out on 18 November?](guides/gta-6-out-18-november.md) — No: GTA 6 comes out on 19 November 2026 in every region, at midnight local time, even though a few store pages show 18 November.

## Price and editions
- [How much is GTA 6 in the UK?](guides/gta-6-price-uk.md) — GTA 6 costs £69.99 for the Standard Edition and £89.99 for the Ultimate Edition in the UK.
- [How much is GTA 6 in the US?](guides/gta-6-price-us.md) — GTA 6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition in the US.
- [What do you get in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?](guides/gta-6-ultimate-edition.md) — The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition gives you the base game plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade, an exclusive set of items spread across Jason and Lucia's story.
- [Is it too late to get the GTA 6 pre-order bonus?](guides/gta-6-pre-order-bonus.md) — No, it is not too late as of 18 November 2026: Rockstar says digital pre-orders and purchases of either edition made before 20 November 2026 get the bonus, while Xbox and some PlayStation pages word the cut-off as 19 November 2026.
- [Are GTA 6 codes region locked?](guides/gta-6-region-locked-codes.md) — GTA 6 codes for PlayStation are region locked, but Xbox codes are not.

## Platforms and download
- [Is GTA 6 on PC?](guides/gta-6-pc.md) — A PC version of GTA 6 is not confirmed yet.
- [Can you play GTA 6 on PS4?](guides/gta-6-ps4.md) — No, GTA 6 is not playable on PS4; on PlayStation it is PS5 only.
- [Can you play GTA 6 on Xbox One?](guides/gta-6-xbox-one.md) — You can play GTA 6 on Xbox One consoles through Xbox Cloud Gaming streaming; there is no native Xbox One version listed.
- [How do I pre-load GTA 6?](guides/gta-6-how-to-pre-load.md) — GTA 6 digital pre-load opened on 10 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and code-in-box copies can be redeemed and used to pre-load as soon as you have them.
- [How many GB is GTA 6?](guides/gta-6-file-size.md) — GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB of free space to pre-load, depending on your device, according to Rockstar's pre-load FAQ.
- [What languages is GTA 6 in?](guides/gta-6-languages.md) — On PlayStation, GTA 6 has English audio only, with on-screen text in 12 listed languages; the Xbox store shows 13 supported languages but does not name them.

## Performance
- [Is GTA 6 60fps?](guides/gta-6-fps.md) — A 60fps mode for GTA 6 is not confirmed yet; Rockstar says the game targets 30 frames per second on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- [What does PS5 Pro do for GTA 6?](guides/gta-6-ps5-pro.md) — On PS5 Pro, GTA 6 gets sharper image clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows, which are only on PS5 Pro.

## Modes
- [Does GTA 6 have GTA Online?](guides/gta-6-online.md) — GTA Online for GTA 6 is not confirmed yet.
