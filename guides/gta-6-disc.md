# Does GTA 6 come with a disc?

No: the physical version of GTA 6 is a download code in a box, with no disc.

- **What is in the box:** a download code for the game plus the Vintage Vice City Pack.
- **When it is in shops:** code-in-box copies are at retailers from 12 November 2026.
- **Pre-loading:** the code can be redeemed and used to pre-load as soon as you receive it. See [How do I pre-load GTA 6?](gta-6-how-to-pre-load.md)
- **PlayStation:** the Standard Edition is sold as a physical code-in-box (no disc) or digitally on PlayStation Store. The Ultimate Edition is sold only on PlayStation Store.
- **Physical Ultimate Edition:** not confirmed yet.
- **A disc release:** not confirmed yet. Asked about a physical disc, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick did not confirm one and said such news comes from Rockstar (per TweakTown).

PlayStation codes only work on accounts from the region they were sold for: see [Are GTA 6 codes region locked?](gta-6-region-locked-codes.md)
