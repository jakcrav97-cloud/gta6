# How do I upgrade GTA 6 to the Ultimate Edition?

Buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade at any point after your Standard Edition download code is redeemed; on PlayStation this can only be done through PlayStation Store.

- **Who can upgrade:** Standard owners, including people who bought a physical copy (the physical version is a download code in a box).
- **PlayStation:** upgrading either Standard version (digital or physical code) to Ultimate can only be done through PlayStation Store (PlayStation UK support page).
- **Xbox price:** £20.00 add-on on Microsoft Store UK; $20.00 add-on on Microsoft Store US.
- **PlayStation price and other regions:** not confirmed yet.
- **Step-by-step menu instructions:** not confirmed yet.
- **GTA+ month:** on PlayStation Store US, buying the Ultimate Edition Upgrade on its own does not qualify for the one-month GTA+ bonus.

What the upgrade adds: see [What do you get in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?](gta-6-ultimate-edition.md)
