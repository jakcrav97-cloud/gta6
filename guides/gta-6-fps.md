# Is GTA 6 60fps?

A 60fps mode for GTA 6 is not confirmed yet; Rockstar says the game targets 30 frames per second on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

- **Frame rate:** Rockstar says GTA 6 targets 30 frames per second on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **60fps or performance mode:** not confirmed yet.
- **PS5 Pro:** Rockstar lists sharper image clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows that are only on PS5 Pro. PlayStation Store shows a "PS5 Pro Enhanced" label.
- **Frame rate on PS5 Pro:** not confirmed yet.
