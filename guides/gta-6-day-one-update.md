# Does GTA 6 have a day one update?

A GTA 6 day-one update is not confirmed yet.

- **Update version number:** not confirmed yet.
- **Update download size:** not confirmed yet.
- **Patch notes:** not confirmed yet. Rockstar has published no patch notes that are in our sources.
- **What we don't use:** press and update-tracker reports are not official, so this page does not repeat their numbers.

## What is confirmed about the download

- The pre-load needs up to 212 GB of free space, depending on the device (Rockstar's pre-load FAQ). More: [How many GB is GTA 6?](gta-6-file-size.md)
- For an automatic pre-load, PS5 needs auto-downloads on and the console on or in Rest Mode; Xbox needs automatic updates on and the console on or in Sleep mode.
- On PlayStation, PlayStation UK sends gameplay, patch and bug issues to Rockstar Support.
