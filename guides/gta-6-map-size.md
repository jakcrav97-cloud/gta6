# How big is the GTA 6 map?

The size of the GTA 6 map is not confirmed yet.

Rockstar's "Only in Leonida" page gives no map size. Here is what is confirmed about where the game takes place:

- **Setting:** Vice City, USA, in the state of Leonida.
- **Named destinations:** Rockstar's "Only in Leonida" page names six destinations in Leonida: Vice City, Leonida Keys, Grassrivers, Port Gellhorn, Ambrosia and Mount Kalaga. Rockstar calls them "a few of the must-see destinations", so this is not the full list.
- **What each destination is:** whether each one is a region, a city or something else, plus its size and position on the map: not confirmed yet.
- **Full list of locations:** not confirmed yet.
- **Official Leonida map:** Rockstar will put a map of Leonida on the official site as a free digital download on 19 November 2026, alongside a printable cover-art poster. What the map shows is not confirmed yet, because it is not out yet.
