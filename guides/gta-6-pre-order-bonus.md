# Is it too late to get the GTA 6 pre-order bonus?

No, it is not too late as of 18 November 2026: Rockstar says digital pre-orders and purchases of either edition made before 20 November 2026 get the bonus, while Xbox and some PlayStation pages word the cut-off as 19 November 2026.

## What the bonus is

- **Rockstar's wording:** digital pre-orders and purchases of either edition (Standard or Ultimate) made before 20 November 2026 get the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ Membership.
- **Vintage Vice City Pack:** '55 Vapid Stanier Sedan and Garage, outfits and hairstyles, and an exclusive weapon pattern.
- **Physical copies:** physical boxes include the Vintage Vice City Pack. No separate physical deadline is given.

## Cut-off wording by store

The stores do not all word the deadline the same way:

- **Rockstar:** before 20 November 2026.
- **Xbox (Microsoft Store UK):** purchases before 23:59:59 on 19 November 2026.
- **PlayStation UK:** the promo terms give a 19 November 2026 cut-off, while the PlayStation UK FAQ says "before 20 November".
- **PlayStation Store US and Canada:** digital Standard or Ultimate bought on PlayStation Store by 19 November 2026 qualifies.

## The GTA+ month

- **Xbox:** the GTA+ month must be redeemed within 180 days.
- **PlayStation:** the GTA+ month must be claimed by 31 March 2027. One per account. It renews at the paid rate unless cancelled.
- **Ultimate Edition Upgrade on its own:** does not qualify for the GTA+ month (PlayStation Store US page).
