# What languages is GTA 6 in?

On PlayStation, GTA 6 has English audio only, with on-screen text in 12 listed languages; the Xbox store shows 13 supported languages but does not name them.

## PlayStation (PS5)

- **Audio (voice):** English only.
- **On-screen text (12 listed):** English, French, German, Italian, Korean, Polish, Portuguese (Brazil), Russian, Simplified Chinese, Spanish (Mexico), Spanish (Spain), Traditional Chinese.
- These come from the PlayStation UK support page. The Thailand version of the page lists the same languages. The page does not say whether languages differ by region.

## Xbox (Series X|S)

- Microsoft Store UK, US and India each show "13 supported languages".
- Which 13 languages: not confirmed yet. The store pages do not list the names.

## Total number of languages

- A single total language count across both platforms is not confirmed yet. PlayStation lists 12 text languages plus English audio, Xbox shows 13 unnamed languages, and no source explains the difference.
