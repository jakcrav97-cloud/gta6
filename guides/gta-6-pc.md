# Is GTA 6 on PC?

A PC version of GTA 6 is not confirmed yet.

- **Where PC is missing:** PC is not mentioned on Rockstar's platforms page, not in Take-Two's release lineup, and not listed on Microsoft Store UK or Microsoft Store US.
- **What Take-Two said:** asked about PC, Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick did not confirm it and said such news comes from Rockstar, per TweakTown. In a Bloomberg interview cited by TweakTown, he said Rockstar always starts on console.
- **Confirmed platforms:** PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **Release date:** 19 November 2026, at midnight local time in all regions.
- **PC release date:** not confirmed yet.
