# Can you play GTA 6 offline?

On PS5, PlayStation Store lists GTA 6 with offline play enabled; offline play on Xbox is not confirmed yet.

- **PlayStation:** PlayStation Store pages (including UK, US, Canada and Japan) list GTA VI as 1 player / single player with offline play enabled.
- **Xbox:** Microsoft Store UK, US and India list single-player as the capability. They do not say whether the game can be played offline, so offline play on Xbox is not confirmed yet.
- **Internet and account:** the Australian PlayStation Store says some features may need an internet connection and a Rockstar Games account. Which features: not confirmed yet.
- **Internet to install or download the game:** not confirmed yet.
