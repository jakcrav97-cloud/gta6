# What does PS5 Pro do for GTA 6?

On PS5 Pro, GTA 6 gets sharper image clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR) and ray-traced shadows, which are only on PS5 Pro.

- **PSSR:** PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution gives sharper image clarity on PS5 Pro.
- **Ray-traced shadows:** only on PS5 Pro.
- **Store label:** PlayStation Store shows a "PS5 Pro Enhanced" label for the game.
- **PlayStation.Blog:** Sony's PlayStation.Blog also lists improved visuals on PS5 Pro.
- **"Plays best on":** Rockstar's official page says the game plays best on PlayStation 5.
- **On every PS5 (not only Pro):** DualSense haptic feedback throughout (adjustable in accessibility settings), Tempest 3D Audio spatial audio and improved load times. PlayStation.Blog also lists the DualSense speaker and adaptive triggers, and says the faster loading comes from the PS5 SSD.
- **Frame rate on PS5 Pro:** not confirmed yet. Rockstar and PlayStation.Blog list PS5 Pro image and shadow upgrades but give no PS5 Pro frame rate. Rockstar says the game targets 30 frames per second on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.
