# Can you play GTA 6 on Xbox Cloud Gaming?

Yes, Rockstar says GTA 6 is playable through Xbox Cloud Gaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles.

- **Consoles:** cloud streaming works on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles.
- **Graphics:** the cloud version uses Xbox Series S graphics settings.
- **Connection:** Rockstar says connection speed can affect performance.
- **Xbox One:** there is no native Xbox One version listed (Microsoft Store UK and US list Xbox Series X|S only); it is playable on Xbox One consoles through Xbox Cloud Gaming streaming. More: [Can you play GTA 6 on Xbox One?](gta-6-xbox-one.md)
- **What you need (a subscription, buying the game, or both):** not confirmed yet.
