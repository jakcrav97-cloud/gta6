# Can you play GTA 6 early with the New Zealand trick?

Whether changing your region lets you play GTA 6 early is not confirmed yet; what is confirmed is that the launch starts in New Zealand at 12:00 a.m. NZDT on 19 November 2026.

- **Where the launch starts:** New Zealand, at 12:00 a.m. NZDT on 19 November 2026. The launch then moves around the world as each country reaches midnight on 19 November. Sony's PlayStation.Blog calls it a rolling midnight release, country by country.
- **Launch time:** Rockstar says midnight local time on 19 November 2026, in all regions, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. More: [What time does GTA 6 unlock?](gta-6-unlock-time.md)
- **PlayStation codes are region locked:** PlayStation codes only work on accounts registered in the region they were sold for. Rockstar advises buying from a retailer in the same country as your platform account. More: [Are GTA 6 codes region locked?](gta-6-region-locked-codes.md)
- **Whether changing your console or account region changes when you can play:** not confirmed yet. No confirmed source covers it.
