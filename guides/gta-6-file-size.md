# How many GB is GTA 6?

GTA 6 needs up to 212 GB of free space to pre-load, depending on your device, according to Rockstar's pre-load FAQ.

- **Rockstar's wording:** the pre-load needs up to 212 GB of free space, depending on the device (Rockstar's pre-load FAQ).
- **PlayStation UK's wording:** at least 212 GB free on PS5 (PlayStation UK game page); PlayStation UK support says 212 GB of free storage.
- **Exact size per console:** not confirmed yet.

## Pre-loading

- Digital pre-load opened on 10 November 2026: at midnight local time on PS5, and at 12 a.m. GMT on Xbox Series X|S.
- For an automatic pre-load, PS5 needs auto-downloads on and the console on or in Rest Mode. Xbox needs automatic updates on and the console on or in Sleep mode.
