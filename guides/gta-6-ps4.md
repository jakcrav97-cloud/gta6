# Can you play GTA 6 on PS4?

No, GTA 6 is not playable on PS4; on PlayStation it is PS5 only.

- **PlayStation UK says:** the game is not playable on PS4 and is PS5 only.
- **Platforms:** PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **Release date:** 19 November 2026, at midnight local time in all regions.
- **PS5 Pro:** sharper image clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR), and ray-traced shadows only on PS5 Pro.
