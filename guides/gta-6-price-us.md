# How much is GTA 6 in the US?

GTA 6 costs $79.99 for the Standard Edition and $99.99 for the Ultimate Edition in the US.

- **PlayStation (PS5):** $79.99 Standard, $99.99 Ultimate, on PlayStation Store US.
- **Xbox (Series X|S):** Microsoft Store US shows "$79.99+" and "$99.99+". The store does not explain the "+".
- **Upgrading Standard to Ultimate on Xbox:** the Ultimate Edition Upgrade is a $20.00 add-on on Microsoft Store US.
- **Upgrading Standard to Ultimate on PlayStation:** the upgrade price is not confirmed yet.
- **When you can upgrade:** Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade at any point after their digital download code is redeemed, including physical buyers.
- **GTA+ month on PlayStation Store US:** a digital Standard or Ultimate Edition bought on PlayStation Store by 19 November 2026 qualifies; the Ultimate Edition Upgrade bought on its own does not.
