# What are the GTA 6 review scores?

As of 18 November 2026 at about 18:00 UK time, GTA 6 has an OpenCritic Top Critic Average of 99 from 88 critic reviews and a Metacritic PS5 Metascore of 99; both are snapshots that change as reviews are added.

Review scores are each outlet's opinion of the game, not facts about it.

## Aggregate scores (snapshots, checked 18 November 2026 at about 18:00 UK time)

- **OpenCritic:** Top Critic Average 99, Critics Recommend 99%, from 88 critic reviews at that time.
- **Metacritic, PlayStation 5:** Metascore 99, labelled "Universal Acclaim" with a "must-play" badge.
- **Metacritic PS5 review count:** not confirmed yet. Metacritic's page contradicts itself: the score box says 147, the critic reviews page says it shows 153, and its positive/mixed/negative breakdown adds up to 147.
- **Metacritic Metascore, Xbox Series X|S:** not confirmed yet. No separate Xbox Metascore has been recorded.

## When reviews went up

Critic reviews went up on 18 November 2026. OpenCritic and Metacritic date the reviews they list 18 November 2026.

## Outlet scores (each is that outlet's opinion)

- **Push Square (PS5, Sammy Barker):** Push Square gave it 10/10 ("Outstanding"). The reviewer rates it the deepest and most accomplished open world since Red Dead Redemption 2. OpenCritic also lists Push Square at 10/10.
- **VGC (Jordan Middler):** VGC's own review page gives it 5/5. The reviewer calls it a modern masterpiece, well worth the wait.
- **VGC score conflict:** OpenCritic lists VGC's review as unscored, which does not match the 5/5 on VGC's own page. VGC's own page is the direct source for its score.
- **TheSixthAxis (Aran Suddi):** TheSixthAxis gave it 9/10. The reviewer calls it a great game that lives up to much of the hype and expectation. OpenCritic also lists TheSixthAxis at 9/10.
- **Shacknews (Bill Lavoy):** Shacknews gave it 10/10. The reviewer describes it as a love story set in an unmatched open world and everything fans hoped for. OpenCritic also lists Shacknews at 10/10.
- **IGN (Luke Reilly):** listed by OpenCritic at 10/10. IGN's own review text is not in our sources, so this score comes from the OpenCritic listing only.
