# Does GTA 6 have GTA Online?

GTA Online for GTA 6 is not confirmed yet.

- **Rockstar and Take-Two:** no Rockstar or Take-Two source in our vault mentions GTA Online for GTA 6, either way.
- **What the store pages say:** in their own wording, the official PlayStation and Microsoft store pages describe GTA VI as a single-player experience. PlayStation Store lists 1 player with offline play enabled, and Microsoft Store UK lists "Single player" as the only capability. The PlayStation UK FAQ gives the same answer and lists no multiplayer modes. This is store wording only: it does not confirm or rule out GTA Online for GTA 6.
- **Internet and account:** the Australian PlayStation Store says some features may need an internet connection and a Rockstar Games account.
