# What time does GTA 6 unlock?

GTA 6 unlocks at midnight local time on 19 November 2026, in all regions, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

- **Where this comes from:** Rockstar's support page on regional release times gives midnight local time on 19 November 2026 for all regions, on both consoles.
- **PlayStation UK support:** gives 12:00 a.m. local time on 19 November 2026 for PS5.
- **Where it starts:** New Zealand, at 12:00 a.m. NZDT on 19 November 2026. Sony's PlayStation.Blog calls it a rolling midnight release: the launch then moves around the world as each country reaches midnight on 19 November.
- **One worldwide launch moment (GMT):** not confirmed yet. Rockstar gives local midnight per region, not one global time, and PlayStation.Blog gives no GMT time either.
- **Country-by-country unlock times:** not confirmed yet. Neither Rockstar's regional-times page nor PlayStation.Blog has a table.

For the UK, see [What time does GTA 6 come out in the UK?](gta-6-release-time-uk.md).
