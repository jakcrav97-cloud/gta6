# Who are the main characters in GTA 6?

The main characters in GTA 6 are Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos.

**Story:** Jason and Lucia are pulled into a criminal conspiracy across the state of Leonida after an easy score goes wrong.

- **Jason Duval:** grew up around criminals, served in the Army, then worked for drug runners in the Keys.
- **Lucia Caminos:** served time in Leonida Penitentiary, is now out, and wants a better life.

## Other characters on the official page

Rockstar's "Only in Leonida" page has eight character sections: Jason, Lucia and these six.

- **Cal Hampton:** Jason's friend and an associate of Brian's; listens to Coast Guard radio at home.
- **Boobie Ike:** Vice City figure with legitimate businesses (real estate, a strip club, a recording studio); runs Only Raw Records with Dre'Quan Priest.
- **Dre'Quan Priest:** runs Only Raw Records with Boobie Ike and signs new acts, including Real Dimez.
- **Real Dimez:** rap duo of Bae-Luxe and Roxy, childhood friends, signed to Only Raw Records.
- **Raul Bautista:** experienced bank robber who recruits people for risky jobs.
- **Brian Heder:** veteran drug runner in the Keys who runs a boat yard with his wife Lori; lets Jason live in one of his properties in exchange for help.

**Full cast:** not confirmed yet. Rockstar does not say these eight are the whole cast.
