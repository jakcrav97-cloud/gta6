# What do you get in the GTA 6 Ultimate Edition?

The GTA 6 Ultimate Edition gives you the base game plus the Ultimate Edition Upgrade, an exclusive set of items spread across Jason and Lucia's story.

**Price:** £89.99 in the UK on PlayStation Store UK and the PlayStation UK game page. Microsoft Store UK shows £89.99+ and does not explain the "+".

## What the Ultimate Edition Upgrade includes

Rockstar's editions page lists these 16 items (the page does not give a total count):

- '95 Grotti Cheetah
- Hawk & Little Morgan Revolvers
- Personalized Weapon Variants
- Vice City Styles
- Jason's Safehouse Vehicles
- Ganado Retro Build
- Rideout Customs Mod Shop
- Sara's Unisex Salon
- Shitzu Squalo
- Stock 305 Clothing Store
- '67 Vapid Dominator Buggy and Garage
- Electric Fang Tattoo Parlor
- One-Eyed Willie's Mod Shop
- Goodtime Gear
- PTT Youngin$ Compound
- Classic Car Collection

## Upgrading from Standard

- Standard owners can buy the Ultimate Edition Upgrade at any point after their digital download code is redeemed. This applies to physical buyers too.
- On PlayStation, upgrading either Standard version (digital or physical code) to Ultimate can only be done through PlayStation Store (PlayStation UK support page).
- On Microsoft Store UK the upgrade is a £20.00 add-on, and on Microsoft Store US it is a $20.00 add-on. The upgrade price on PlayStation and in other regions is not confirmed yet.

## Physical copies and bonuses

- **Physical Ultimate Edition:** not confirmed yet. Rockstar's editions page lists none, and PlayStation UK support says Ultimate is sold only on PlayStation Store. No source covers Xbox.
- **Early purchase bonus:** digital pre-orders and purchases of either edition made before 20 November 2026 get the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ Membership.
