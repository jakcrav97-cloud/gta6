# Is GTA 6 out on 18 November?

No: GTA 6 comes out on 19 November 2026 in every region, at midnight local time, even though a few store pages show 18 November.

## Which stores show 18 November

These store pages show an 18 November 2026 date, with no time and no explanation:

- **PlayStation Store Germany:** 18.11.2026
- **PlayStation Store Australia:** 18/11/2026
- **PlayStation Store Japan:** 18 November 2026
- **Microsoft Store India:** 18 November 2026

## Why the answer is 19 November

- Rockstar's own support page on regional release times is an official source, which ranks above platform stores.
- It states midnight local time on 19 November 2026 in all regions, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. That covers Germany, Australia, Japan and India.
- The 18 November store dates do not override Rockstar.
