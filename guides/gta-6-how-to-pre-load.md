# How do I pre-load GTA 6?

GTA 6 digital pre-load opened on 10 November 2026 on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S, and code-in-box copies can be redeemed and used to pre-load as soon as you have them.

## When pre-load opened

- **Digital pre-load:** from 10 November 2026. This was moved earlier than the 12 November date in the June pre-order announcement.
- **PS5:** midnight local time on 10 November 2026.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** 12 a.m. GMT on 10 November 2026, the same moment worldwide.

## Pre-loading automatically

- **PS5:** turn auto-downloads on and keep the console on or in Rest Mode.
- **Xbox Series X|S:** turn automatic updates on and keep the console on or in Sleep mode.
- **Exact menu steps:** not confirmed yet.

## Physical (code-in-box) copies

- Physical copies are a download code in a box, with no disc. They are at retailers from 12 November 2026.
- The code can be redeemed and used to pre-load as soon as you receive it.

## Space you need

- Up to 212 GB of free space, depending on the device. PlayStation UK words it as at least 212 GB free on PS5, and PlayStation UK support says 212 GB of free storage.
- More detail: [How many GB is GTA 6?](gta-6-file-size.md).
