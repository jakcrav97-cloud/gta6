# Is GTA 6 out yet?

GTA 6 is out once it is midnight on 19 November 2026 where you live: it unlocks at midnight local time in every region, in a rolling launch that starts in New Zealand at 12:00 a.m. NZDT.

If it is past midnight on 19 November 2026 where you live, it has unlocked: Rockstar gives midnight local time on 19 November 2026 for all regions, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

- **Platforms:** PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **Rolling launch:** Sony's PlayStation.Blog calls it a rolling midnight release, country by country. It starts in New Zealand and moves around the world as each country reaches midnight on 19 November.
- **Some store pages show 18 November:** PlayStation Store Germany, Australia and Japan and Microsoft Store India show 18 November 2026, with no time and no explanation. Rockstar's official support page says 19 November 2026 in all regions, and that is the date we use. More: [Is GTA 6 out on 18 November?](gta-6-out-18-november.md)
- **One worldwide launch moment (GMT):** not confirmed yet. Rockstar gives local midnight per region, not one global time.
- **Country-by-country unlock table:** not confirmed yet.
- **Rockstar "out now" post:** not confirmed yet. No official launch-day "out now" post is in our sources.
