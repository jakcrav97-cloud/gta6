# How much is GTA 6 in the UK?

GTA 6 costs £69.99 for the Standard Edition and £89.99 for the Ultimate Edition in the UK.

- **PlayStation (PS5):** £69.99 Standard, £89.99 Ultimate, on PlayStation Store UK and the PlayStation UK game page.
- **Xbox (Series X|S):** Microsoft Store UK shows "£69.99+" and "£89.99+". The store does not explain the "+".
- **Upgrading Standard to Ultimate:** the Ultimate Edition Upgrade is a £20.00 add-on on Microsoft Store UK. The PlayStation upgrade price is not confirmed yet.
- **When you can upgrade:** Standard owners can buy the upgrade at any point after their digital download code is redeemed, including physical buyers.
- **Early purchase bonus:** digital purchases of either edition made before 20 November 2026 get the Vintage Vice City Pack and one month of GTA+ Membership. Physical boxes include the pack.

For US prices, see [How much is GTA 6 in the US?](gta-6-price-us.md).
