# Are GTA 6 codes region locked?

GTA 6 codes for PlayStation are region locked, but Xbox codes are not.

- **PlayStation:** codes only work on accounts registered in the region they were sold for. The regions are Brazil; LATAM; Canada/US; Australia/New Zealand; South Korea; Japan; UK; Saudi Arabia/UAE; a Southeast/East Asia group; and Rest of EMEA and Asia.
- **Rockstar's advice:** buy from a retailer in the same country as your platform account.
- **Xbox:** codes are not region-locked.
- **Japan:** product codes expire 170 days after issuance, counted from launch day (19 November 2026), due to local regulations.
