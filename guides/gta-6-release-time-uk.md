# What time does GTA 6 come out in the UK?

GTA 6 comes out at midnight UK local time, as 18 November turns into 19 November 2026, on PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

- **Where this comes from:** Rockstar's support page on regional release times says the game unlocks at midnight local time on 19 November 2026 in all regions, on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. The page does not name the UK specifically; midnight UK time follows from that "all regions" rule.
- **PlayStation UK support:** gives 12:00 a.m. local time on 19 November 2026 for PS5.
- **Release date:** 19 November 2026. PlayStation Store UK and Microsoft Store UK show 19/11/2026, and the PlayStation UK game page shows 19 November 2026.
- **One worldwide launch moment (GMT):** not confirmed yet. Rockstar gives midnight local time per region, not one global time.
- **Country-by-country unlock times:** not confirmed yet. Rockstar's regional-times page has no table.

## Pre-load

- Digital pre-load opened on 10 November 2026: at midnight local time on PS5, and at 12 a.m. GMT on Xbox Series X|S.
- Code-in-box copies can be redeemed and used to pre-load as soon as you have them.
