# When is the GTA 6 release date?

GTA 6 comes out on 19 November 2026 on PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.

- **Time of day:** midnight local time on 19 November 2026, in all regions, on both consoles (Rockstar's support page on regional release times).
- **Who confirms it:** Rockstar's support page, Rockstar's official GTA VI page, Rockstar's pre-order press release and Take-Two's Q2 FY2027 earnings release all give 19 November 2026.
- **Store pages:** PlayStation Store UK, PlayStation Store Ireland and Microsoft Store UK show 19/11/2026. The PlayStation UK game page, PlayStation Store US, PlayStation Store Canada and Microsoft Store US show 19 November 2026.
- **Why some stores show 18 November:** PlayStation Store Germany (18.11.2026), PlayStation Store Australia (18/11/2026), PlayStation Store Japan (18 November 2026) and Microsoft Store India (18 November 2026) show 18 November, with no time and no explanation. Rockstar's support page is the official source and says midnight local time on 19 November 2026 in all regions, which covers Germany, Australia, Japan and India.
- **Earlier dates:** the game moved from a 2025 window to 26 May 2026, then to 19 November 2026 (GTABoom).
- **Pre-load:** digital pre-load opened on 10 November 2026.
