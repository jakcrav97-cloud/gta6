# Is GTA 6 better on PS5 or Xbox?

Whether GTA 6 is better on PS5 or Xbox is not confirmed yet, but Rockstar says it plays best on PlayStation 5, and it targets 30 frames per second on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S.

- **"Plays best on":** Rockstar's official page says the game plays best on PlayStation 5.
- **Frame rate:** Rockstar says the game targets 30 frames per second on both PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. A 60fps or performance mode is not confirmed yet. More: [Is GTA 6 60fps?](gta-6-fps.md)
- **PS5 features:** DualSense haptic feedback throughout (adjustable in accessibility settings), Tempest 3D Audio spatial audio and improved load times. PlayStation.Blog also lists the DualSense speaker and adaptive triggers, and says the faster loading comes from the PS5 SSD.
- **PS5 Pro:** sharper image clarity through PlayStation Spectral Super Resolution (PSSR), and ray-traced shadows that are only on PS5 Pro. PlayStation Store shows a "PS5 Pro Enhanced" label. More: [What does PS5 Pro do for GTA 6?](gta-6-ps5-pro.md)
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming:** on Xbox, the game can also be played through cloud streaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles, using Xbox Series S graphics settings. Connection speed can affect performance.
- **Side-by-side comparison of image quality or performance:** not confirmed yet.
