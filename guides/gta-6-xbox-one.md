# Can you play GTA 6 on Xbox One?

You can play GTA 6 on Xbox One consoles through Xbox Cloud Gaming streaming; there is no native Xbox One version listed.

- **No native version:** Microsoft Store UK and US list Xbox Series X|S only.
- **Xbox Cloud Gaming:** Rockstar says the game is playable via cloud streaming on Xbox Series X|S and Xbox One consoles, using Xbox Series S graphics settings. Connection speed can affect performance.
- **Confirmed platforms:** PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S.
- **What you need to play through the cloud (subscription or purchase):** not confirmed yet.
